# MailGester brand

**Name:** MailGester, one word. A jester for your mail, and a digest of it.

**Tagline:** Never miss the mail that matters.

**Security line:** Private by design: local AI, encrypted credentials.

## Logo

An envelope wearing a three horn jester hat with bells. Files are in `web/public/`: `logo.svg`, `favicon.svg`, `logo-512.png`.

## Wordmark

`Mail` in the text colour, `Gester` in the accent blue. Bold, system fonts, no external font loading.

## Palette

| Role | Colour | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#1d2433` | Outlines, dark surface |
| Paper | `#f6f3ec` | Light surface |
| Blue | `#3b6fe0` | Main accent, quiet Discord posts |
| Coral | `#e5533d` | Needs attention, pinged posts |
| Gold | `#f2b632` | Caution, junk |

The UI uses a slightly deeper coral and gold in the light theme, and a lighter blue in the dark theme, for text contrast.

## Voice

Dry and plain. The jester lives in the logo and the tagline. Errors and settings text stay serious.
